RAWmakase 0.4.10 can fit a look to a RAW's own embedded JPEG, so a photo starts closer to what the camera showed, and photos with no colour data at all now start from that look instead of raw camera colour. Select Sky has a new model that leaves fewer gaps beside poles and heads and no longer mistakes as much sea for sky. Failures and running work now appear in the top bar, where they are hard to miss, and Windows falls back to another graphics API when a DirectX 12 device fails to start.

## New

- **Fit Camera Look.** **Profile ▸ Fit Camera Look** fits a look to the open RAW's embedded JPEG, the camera's own rendering of the photo, in a few seconds, and uses it as the profile, one History step. It appears as **Camera Look (fitted)**, with the name of the camera's look where it can be read: Fujifilm film simulations, Sony creative styles, Nikon Picture Controls and Canon Picture Styles, such as "Camera Look (fitted): Classic Chrome". The fit travels with the edit in XMP sidecars, presets and JPEG exports, and Sync and Copy use the source photo's fit. Each fit is checked against the JPEG on parts of the photo it did not learn from, and refused with the reason if it does not line up or does not improve on the starting point. RAWs with no usable JPEG preview, previews that do not state their colour space, JPEG XL previews and DNGs written by Adobe software cannot be fitted. In Lightroom the fitted profile's name is unknown, so it falls back. `rawmakase fit-look` does the same from the command line. ([#765](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/765))
- **A starting look for photos without colour data.** A photo with no colour matrix, no embedded profile and no imported camera profile now starts from its fitted look, in Develop, Reset, the Library, export, Sync and `rawmakase render`, where its JPEG allows a fit. The fit runs once per file and is remembered. Every other photo keeps its current default and renders exactly as before. ([#765](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/765))
- **Camera Thumbnail.** **Shift+\\** or **Photo ▸ Camera Thumbnail** shows the embedded JPEG in Develop, labelled, in place of the photo. ([#765](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/765))

## Improved

- **Select Sky has its own model.** Sky now uses UperNet ConvNeXt, which leaves fewer blocky gaps beside poles and heads and takes less of the sea for sky. Subject and Background are unchanged. The first Sky asks to download the 243 MB model; Subject installs from earlier releases stay valid. It is slower, about 5.5 s rather than 3.3 s per Sky on 8 CPU threads, and it is not better everywhere: dark rock or ceilings can read as night sky, a sea lost in haze can be selected, and thin blown-out gaps of sky can be missed, sometimes ending in "No sky found". ([#772](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/772))
- **Failures and running work show in the top bar.** Messages used to go to a small line at the bottom of the window, and in the Library some were hidden altogether. Results now show in the top bar for a few seconds, warnings for at least 10 seconds, and failures stay until dismissed. Click the line to read every message in full and copy a failure's details; **Shift+Esc** or the cross dismisses them. Long work such as Auto, Upright, Lensfun downloads, Read Metadata and imports shows its progress there too. Photos that cannot be opened in Develop no longer stop you with a dialog, and export problems are listed in the same place instead of a separate window. ([#769](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/769))

## Fixed

- **Windows falls back when DirectX 12 fails to start.** RAWmakase now tries creating a device before opening the window, in the order DirectX 12, Vulkan, OpenGL, and the fatal error names the call that failed instead of only "Parent device is lost". Tested with a forced DirectX 12 failure in a virtual machine, where it opened through OpenGL; it has not yet been confirmed on real hardware that showed the problem. Failures later than device creation can still stop the app. ([#770](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/770), [#768](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/768))
- **Thumbnails stay after a manual profile import.** Importing a camera profile or LUT from a file or folder no longer drops the open photo's thumbnail when its edit did not change, which could leave a grey placeholder for a RAW with no embedded preview. The Import Adobe profiles shortcuts still have this problem ([#766](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/766)). ([#767](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/pull/767), [#764](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/764))

## Upgrade notes

- Unedited photos with no colour data change appearance when they get a fitted look, and their previews rebuild. Importing any profile also rebuilds previews of unedited photos.
- Named white balance presets are disabled under a fitted base, for those same photos. As Shot, Auto, the selector and Temp and Tint still work.
- Existing Sky masks keep their pixels until you regenerate them.
- If you use a remote selection worker, update it together with the app: the protocol changed, and an old worker and a new app refuse each other.
- Export notes now go away by themselves after about 10 seconds, and separate exports report separately instead of adding up into one summary.

## Known limitations

- Fitted looks are measured against the camera's JPEG, not Camera Raw. On a private set of 139 RAWs, 118 were fitted, with a median whole-image error of ΔE00 1.01 against each JPEG. Skin hue error rose as the cost of removing bands, Panasonic GH5S skin got worse in the fitted look during this work, and one Sony a7R VI frame is refused without a confirmed cause ([#773](https://github.com/pch/rawmakase/issues/773)).
- The camera's look name is shown in the profile's title only, not yet in the metadata panel or Loupe Info. Sony contrast and Olympus and Panasonic look names are not read.
